### Listening 2 — Interview

Interviewer: Welcome to our student voices programme. Today we are speaking with Maha about the community radio project. Why was this project started? Maha: We wanted to help our school in a practical way, not just read about problems. Students suggested ideas, and our teachers helped us organise a small team. Interviewer: Tell us about your first activity. Maha: The first episode was recorded in the old science classroom. We chose something manageable and listened to feedback from other students. Interviewer: When did everything begin? Maha: The team began in October. We prepared the materials together and agreed on simple responsibilities. Interviewer: Who can get involved? Maha: Students from Grades Eight and Nine can apply. Everyone needs to show respect and take turns. Interviewer: When does the group meet? Maha: They meet every Tuesday after school. We try to finish each session with a useful plan for the next one. Interviewer: What is the most important lesson? Maha: They learned how to check a story before sharing it. It has also helped us become more confident when explaining our ideas. Interviewer: Would you recommend joining? Maha: Yes. Try something small, listen to others, and keep improving. Thank you for inviting me. We are proud that young people can lead meaningful changes, even when resources are limited and progress takes time.
