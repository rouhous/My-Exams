# Grade 9 — QA and originality audit

## Scope
- Four independent models A–D, 7 tasks each, 40 marks, 120 minutes.
- Four combined Grammar & Vocabulary cloze activities (10 gaps, four options).
- Four Reading 1 matching tasks (4 people, 5 statements), four Reading 2 evaluative passages (6 short-answer questions).
- Eight original listening scripts and eight provisional synthetic MP3 recordings.
- Twenty-four newly generated grayscale pictograms reused in 60 answer slots across the 4 Listening 1 activities.
- Narrative and email writing activities (100-word minimum).

## Length and distribution checks

- Model A: L1 5 × 40–50 words; L2 220 words; G/V 72 words after completing gaps; Reading 2 273 words; marks 40.
- Model B: L1 5 × 40–50 words; L2 216 words; G/V 76 words after completing gaps; Reading 2 272 words; marks 40.
- Model C: L1 5 × 40–50 words; L2 216 words; G/V 73 words after completing gaps; Reading 2 279 words; marks 40.
- Model D: L1 5 × 40–50 words; L2 214 words; G/V 70 words after completing gaps; Reading 2 279 words; marks 40.

## Source separation
- The Grade 9 supplied booklet was consulted for component structure, not reused as question or illustration content.
- All exam texts were authored anew for this package. Illustration source: two newly generated mock-screen images created for this project, cropped into grayscale question-option illustrations.
- A basic ten-word exact contiguous overlap check against the supplied booklet extract reported zero matches across the new listening, cloze and long reading texts; this is not a guarantee of global originality.

## Remaining review before official release
- Provisional espeak-generated narration should be replaced by natural speaker recordings; speaker-role variety in interviews remains limited.
- A trained teacher should review phonetic clarity, all answer keys, and accept equivalent accurate short answers in Reading 2.
- Browser navigation and audio playback were syntax-checked; automated browser execution was blocked by the environment, so mobile/print QA is still required.
- These are original mock exams matching the task structure, not official Ministry documents.
